# PokeScan roadmap

Ideas we want to build, roughly in order of how much they would make someone pay for Pro. The ones already
advertised on the Pro page ("Coming to Pro") come first.

## Coming to Pro (announced on the Pro page)

- **Replay what-ifs.** A logged battle re-run with a different lead or one swap, so you see what would have won.
  Needs: the timeline and shield counts per battle (have them), the matchup matrix per scenario (have it), and a
  small simulator that plays the lead exchange and the switches forward from the recording.
- **Season Wrapped.** One shareable card at season end: your record, the team that carried, your nemesis lead,
  the habit you fixed. Needs: the battle log (have it) and a card image renderer (canvas → PNG, share sheet).
- **Share anything: storage grid and raid lobby.** A storage screenshot fills the roster; a raid lobby screenshot
  picks counters from your own Pokémon. Needs: Professor Cedar's vision call to return a list of Pokémon with CP
  from the grid, and the raid page to take a boss from a screenshot.
- **Storage cleanup.** From a storage screenshot: duplicates, dead IV ranks, what to keep for Ultra League.
  Needs: the storage read above plus the roster's spare-copy logic.

## Ideas from building the battle coach and Saved teams

- **Professor Cedar's weekly letter.** A short weekly summary of your habits: what improved, what keeps coming
  back, one drill for the week. Needs: the habits card's counts per week and one review call a week.
- **Habit drills on Today.** Put the top habit ("2 charged moves thrown into a resist") on the Today page with its
  drill, and tick it off when the next battles no longer show it.
- **"How to beat their lead" pages.** For each lead you keep losing to: who in your roster wins it, with which
  shields, and what to switch to. Needs: the battle log's lead records and the matchup matrix (have both).
- **Fast moves in the battle check.** The game does not announce fast moves, so the check only judges charged
  moves. Reading the energy bars or the damage would let it judge fast-move choice too.
- **Candy from the storage screen.** Read candy and stardust off more screens than the status screen, so Saved
  teams knows your resources without a screenshot of every family.
- **Affordable with transfers.** Count the candy you would get by transferring spare copies, and say which ones
  to transfer to afford a power-up.
- **XL candy planning.** For a trio that needs XL (L41+), how many walks, trades or catches it takes.
