How to use PokeScan ✕

Everything runs on your phone. Scans, roster and settings live in this browser's storage and never leave the device.

1 · Scan your Pokémon

  • In Pokémon GO open a Pokémon's status screen and take a screenshot, or screen-record while swiping through your storage.
  • Set your trainer level once, tap ＋ Import scans, pick the screenshots or the recording.
  • Each card shows the solved IVs, Great/Ultra League IV rank, the level and stardust needed to reach the 1500 cap, PvPoke meta rank, and what its evolution would be.
  • Exact IVs: also screenshot (or record) the appraisal screen with the three orange bars. It is matched to the card with the same species, CP and HP and pins the IVs; it never creates a second card. Order does not matter: an appraisal seen first waits for its status screen.
  • Rank chips read like Poké Genie: GL #91 · 99.1% is the IV rank among the 4096 spreads of that species at the 1500 cap and its stat product relative to rank 1.
  • Tap a card to correct species, CP, HP or level and re-solve. ✕ deletes it.
  • Scans stay until you delete them. Rescan a Pokémon only after you power it up or evolve it, and delete the old card.

2 · Build teams

  • Owned is filled from scans at or under 1500 CP, one copy per species. Fix the three moves per Pokémon; they default to what the screenshot showed or PvPoke's recommendation.
  • Pending are pieces one step away: evolutions of scanned Pokémon appear here by themselves, add stones/TM targets yourself.
  • Candidates are wild pickups you consider. Marginal value shows which one moves the needle.
  • Tagged teams are your in-game parties, scored as-is.
  • Scores are on PvPoke's 0–1000 battle-rating scale: the best meta trios score 690–720, an average trio about 520. "No answer to X" means nobody on the team beats X.
  • No scans yet? Load saved roster pulls the roster committed in the repo. Export roster JSON writes the file the Python scripts use.

3 · Data and updates

  • PvPoke rankings are bundled with the app and refreshed automatically every Monday. The date is shown at the top of Team builder.
  • The app works offline after the first visit. Scanning itself needs a connection for the text-recognition library.
  • Install it: on iPhone use Share → Add to Home Screen. Note that the home-screen app has its own storage: move scans with Backup and Restore.
  • Team scores are a heuristic (PvPoke's published matchups plus type effectiveness), not a battle simulation. Verify a team in PvPoke's Team Builder before investing stardust.
